Exam Leader / Maker System

How VeriTest standardizes evaluations across multiple professors and student sections.

The Academic Challenge

When 2 or more professors teach different sections of the same course (e.g. CS-101 Sec A, B, C), having each professor write their own exam causes unequal difficulty, subjective grading bias, and complaints of student unfairness.

The VeriTest Solution

The University Administrator appoints one Exam Leader (Lead Maker) for the course. The Leader designs a single authoritative Master Exam, which VeriTest automatically distributes to all co-professors for uniform deployment.

The 4-Phase System Workflow

1 Admin Appointment

Department Chair / Admin selects all faculty assigned to the course and elects 1 Professor as the Exam Leader ⭐.

2 Master Exam Authoring

The Exam Leader authors the master questions, specifies syllabus competencies, sets time limits, and marks it as Institutional Master Exam.

3 Automated Distribution

VeriTest broadcasts the Master Exam to all co-professors. Co-professors receive a notification badge: "Master Exam Distributed by [Leader]".

4 Sync & Section Grading

Co-professors click Sync & Deploy to release the exam to their section. Each professor independently monitors their students and converts results to official grades.
